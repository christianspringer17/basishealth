What to expect

Eligibility Screening and Clinician Review

Your intake form gives clinicians foundational data to determine whether GLP-1 therapy is right for you. No rubber stamp prescriptions here. Clinician approval for treatment will always prioritize your health and well-being.

Personalized GLP-1 Protocol

No two protocols look the same. We will work with you to select the GLP-1 therapy clinically appropriate for your unique goals and biology, with specific care given to your starting dose and titration schedule.

Ongoing Adaptive Care

We closely monitor your progress, adjusting your GLP-1 dosage as needed. Regular check-ins allow us to fine-tune your treatment, ensuring it aligns with your body's changing needs. GLP-1 therapy works best when the protocol evolves with you.

Metabolic Literacy

GLP–One™ is more than a prescription. It’s a care framework designed to help you address the root of metabolic dysfunction. Easy-to-consume education and personalized clinician counseling are an important part of this process. We want you to understand your metabolism so you can play an active role in its recovery.

The science has changed. Your care should, too.

Your metabolism isn’t one thing. It’s a conversation between systems. New research has given us five markers to tell us how well that conversation is flowing. Our metabolism-first approach to wellness integrates each of these signals into your care.

The Five Metabolic Markers

.

Insulin Sensitivity

Hormonal Response

V02 Max

We focus on the signals that truly predict long-term health. By monitoring key metabolic markers, we gain a clearer picture of how your body responds to treatment, supporting more precise and sustainable health outcomes.

Your insulin sensitivity measures how effectively your cells respond to insulin and absorb glucose from the bloodstream. Improving it supports stable energy levels, better metabolic health, and reduced risk of metabolic disorders.

Muscle mass refers to the total amount of muscle tissue in your body. Increasing it improves strength, power output, metabolic rate, and long-term functional health.

Your hormonal response is how your body releases and regulates hormones during and after exercise. Optimizing it supports muscle growth, recovery, energy balance, and overall metabolic health.

Your V02 max, or maximal oxygen consumption, indicates the maximum amount of oxygen your body can utilize during exercise. Improving it enhances endurance and overall fitness.

Your body fat percentage represents the proportion of fat mass relative to total body weight. Maintaining a healthy range supports metabolic function, hormonal balance, and athletic performance.
