# Designing a Conversion-Focused Homepage for Basis

## Executive summary

The research supports a **homepage architecture change**, not a rebrand. Basis already has the right strategic lane: a premium, gender-neutral longevity brand launching with clinician-guided tirzepatide. The weakest part of the current system is not the brand itself; it is that the live homepage is still more philosophy-led than offer-led, while Basis’s own `/glp-one` page is already much clearer about product, price, approval, and what happens next. citeturn3view1turn3view0

Basal proves that a premium editorial brand can still convert if the homepage introduces the live product immediately and the product page surfaces pricing clearly. Ascnd proves that strong conversion comes from explicit offer language, visible pricing, “what’s included,” and a specific eligibility CTA. Fuse is useful as a **storefront-logic** reference, but not as a consumer-facing visual benchmark: it is an operator platform selling white-label infrastructure, not a premium B2C care brand. citeturn4view4turn4view0turn16view3turn5view4turn8view0turn10view0

The best move is this: make the **current homepage the `/our-approach` page**, build a new `/` that is a **conversion-oriented brand homepage**, and keep `/glp-one` as the deeper plan page for product detail and plan comparison. That structure aligns with NN/g’s guidance that homepages must quickly communicate purpose, site offerings, and top tasks, and with Baymard’s finding that first-time users infer site scope from homepage content and imagery. citeturn27view0turn20view0turn20view2turn23view1

The new homepage should be understood in one short glance. In practice, that means the first screen must show: **what Basis is, what is available now, what it costs from, whether there is provider review, and what the primary next action is**. Research shows users often leave pages within 10–20 seconds, and the first 10 seconds are especially critical for deciding whether to continue. citeturn21view0

The right synthesis is:

- **Basal’s restraint and premium tone**
- **Ascnd’s offer clarity and pricing logic**
- **Basis’s longevity/protocol umbrella**
- **One dominant live offer**
- **Muted future tracks below, not a giant catalog** citeturn4view4turn4view0turn16view3turn5view4turn23view1turn27view0

## Recommended homepage architecture

The new homepage should satisfy the one-glance test that NN/g describes: users should be able to tell **where they are, what the company does, and what they can do next** without hunting. The homepage should also show the live offer through examples, not only through abstract brand narrative. At the same time, NN/g warns against giving too many top-priority tasks equal prominence, while Baymard shows that first-time visitors still need enough breadth to understand what the broader company is about. That combination leads to a simple rule for Basis: **one dominant live offer, three muted expansion tracks.** citeturn27view0turn20view2turn23view1

### What must appear above the fold

The first screen should show **five things at once**: the brand promise, the live offer, a from-price, a provider-approval cue, and a specific CTA. This is the single biggest change from the current homepage. NN/g’s homepage research says users need clarity about goals and offerings immediately, and its studies on generic “Get Started” CTAs show that vague or overly dominant CTAs can pull people into the wrong path before they understand the service. Basis should therefore avoid a generic “Start assessment” label on the homepage and use a more explicit CTA such as **Start GLP-One assessment**. citeturn20view0turn21view0turn20view4turn20view7turn22view0

This also argues against a giant fullscreen hero image, video, or carousel that pushes pricing or offer details below the fold. NN/g documents how prominent generic CTAs on fullscreen hero banners create an illusion of completeness and discourage users from scrolling to the core details beneath. Baymard separately finds that homepage carousels frequently create usability problems and that static alternatives often perform just as well with less risk. citeturn20view3turn23view3

### Site architecture recommendation

The cleanest structure is:

- **`/`** = new conversion-focused brand homepage  
- **`/our-approach`** = current homepage content, refined as the philosophy/brand page  
- **`/glp-one`** = current detailed product page and plan comparison page citeturn3view1turn3view0

That preserves the work already done, avoids a rushed rebrand, and gives paid traffic two rational landing destinations to test. This is the least disruptive architecture change and the most consistent with both the live Basis inventory and homepage usability guidance. citeturn20view0turn27view0turn3view1turn3view0

## Visual design system

The visual system should preserve Basis’s current premium clinical identity while increasing contrast, action emphasis, and first-screen clarity. First impressions affect perceived credibility, usability, and relevance, so the visual changes should improve comprehension—not make the site feel louder or cheaper. citeturn19view7turn20view8

## Wireframes and user journey

Below are three homepage directions. All three are compatible with the Basis brand. The difference is how much of the offer is embedded into the first screen.

### Variant A

A restrained, editorial homepage with the offer preview immediately below the hero.

```text
[ NAV: Basis | GLP-One | How it works | Our Approach | FAQ | Start assessment ]

[ Eyebrow: Basis Metabolic ]
[ H1: Longevity tailored for your biology.                      ][ Product render ]
[ Subhead: Clinician-guided GLP-One tirzepatide care...        ][ clean still life ]
[ Primary CTA: Start GLP-One assessment ]
[ Secondary CTA: View plans ]
[ Microcopy: Provider review required. Only charged if approved. ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ OFFER BAND ]
[ Available now | GLP-One Tirzepatide | From $183/mo | 3-month programs ]
[ Personalized dosing support | Ongoing care | Discreet shipping ]
[ CTA: Compare plans ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ HOW IT WORKS – 4 steps ]
[ WHY BASIS – 3 cards ]
[ FUTURE TRACKS – muted cards ]
[ FAQ PREVIEW ]
[ FINAL CTA ]
```

**Use when:** you want the homepage to stay more editorial and less sales-card-driven.  
**Risk:** on some mobile viewports, price clarity falls below the fold.

### Variant B

A brand-forward hero with an embedded offer card in the first screen.

```text
[ NAV: Basis | GLP-One | How it works | Our Approach | FAQ | Start assessment ]

[ LEFT: BRAND COPY ]                                [ RIGHT: OFFER CARD ]

[ Secondary CTA: View plans ]                       [ Provider review required ]
[ Microcopy: Only charged if approved ]             [ bullets x4 ]
                                                    [ CTA: Compare plans ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ TRUST STRIP: Licensed provider review | U.S. pharmacy fulfillment | Ongoing support ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ PLAN CARDS: Microdose | Weight Loss ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ HOW IT WORKS ]
[ WHY BASIS ]
[ FUTURE TRACKS ]
[ FAQ ]
[ FINAL CTA ]
```

### Variant C

A more product-led homepage that behaves like a premium product landing page.

```text
[ NAV ]

[ H1: GLP-One Tirzepatide ]
[ Subhead: Clinician-guided care with personalized dosing support... ]
[ Price: From $183/mo on 3-month programs ]
[ CTA: Start GLP-One assessment ]
[ Approval microcopy ]

[ HERO MEDIA ]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[ PLAN CARDS ]
[ WHAT'S INCLUDED ]
[ HOW IT WORKS ]
[ WHY BASIS / LONGEVITY FRAMEWORK ]
[ FUTURE TRACKS ]
[ FAQ ]
```

**Use when:** conversion volume matters more than brand breadth in the short term.  
**Risk:** this moves Basis closest to the generic GLP-1 market.

### Preferred variant

**Variant B is the best option.** It gives Basis the strongest balance of brand and conversion because it puts the live offer, price signal, provider-approval cue, and specific CTA in the first screen without turning the whole homepage into a commodity product page. It also follows NN/g’s guidance that homepages should guide users with clarity while reflecting both brand identity and site offerings. It avoids the “hero only” ambiguity that can cause users to stop exploring, and it lets you keep future longevity tracks lower on the page as expansion proof rather than competing offers. citeturn20view0turn20view2turn21view0turn20view3turn27view0
