## Children Under the Age of 18

Where we process the Personal Information of minors we require the subscriber or person inputting the Personal Information to be the parent or guardian of the minor or to obtain their consent. No one under age 18 may provide any information to, or on the Website. Basis does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you are under 18, do not use or provide any information on the Website or on or through any of its features, register on the Website, attempt to make any purchases through the Website, use any of the interactive or public comment features of this Website or provide any information about yourself to Basis or on the Website, including your name, address, telephone number, e-mail address or any screen name or user name you may use. If Basis learns that it has collected or received Personal Information from or about a child under 18 without verification of parental consent, Basis will delete that information. If you believe Basis might have any information from or about a child under 18, please contact us as set forth in the Contact Information section at the end of this Privacy Policy.
