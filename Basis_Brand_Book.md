## 2. Positioning

**Positioning statement (internal):**
For health-motivated adults who want to start GLP-1 the right way, **Basis** is the premium telehealth brand that delivers physician-guided GLP-1 care as a structured protocol — real provider review, personalized dosing, and ongoing support. Unlike cheap access sites that hand out a script and disappear, Basis treats GLP-1 as a guided protocol and feels like premium care, not a vending machine.

**Category / frame of reference:** premium online GLP-1 / metabolic care (not "weight-loss pills," not a med-spa, not a longevity clinic).

**Taglines by context:**
- Hero / brand: *Premium GLP-1 care, guided by licensed providers.*
- Sharp/performance: *GLP-1 care, done properly.*
- Descriptive/SEO: *Physician-guided GLP-1 and longevity protocols, prescribed online if clinically appropriate.*

**Scope guardrail:** Phase 1 leads with GLP-1/metabolic. Peptides, hormone, recovery, and longevity appear only as light, eligibility-gated secondary tracks. No labs/biomarker/portal/treatment-architecture hero language yet.

## 3. Audience

### Primary persona — "The Frustrated Optimizer"
- **Who:** 30s–50s, household income ~$90k+, time-poor, has tried diet/exercise and plateaued; has heard of or considered GLP-1.
- **Wants:** to lose weight in a way that's legitimate, medically supervised, convenient, and discreet — and to not feel like they're buying from a sketchy site.
- **Values:** credibility, a real provider, clean experience, transparent pricing, privacy. Will pay a premium for trust and structure over the cheapest option.
- **Fears:** scams / counterfeit meds, unmanaged side effects, hidden fees, being judged, wasting money on something that won't work for them.
- **Buying triggers:** a brand that looks legitimate and premium; clear "is this right for me" path; provider involvement; obvious, no-surprise pricing; fast start.
- **Top objections (the site must answer):** Is this legit? Will I qualify? Is it safe? What does it really cost? Do I have to commit? What happens after I submit?

### Secondary segment — "Performance/Longevity-curious" (Phase 2 lean)
Biohacker-adjacent, interested in peptides/longevity/hormone optimization. Keep them warm via the secondary tracks, but do not build the site around them in Phase 1.

### Jobs to be done
1. *Functional:* get a legitimate, provider-reviewed GLP-1 prescription delivered, simply.
2. *Emotional:* feel in control and taken seriously, not embarrassed or scammed.
3. *Social:* be able to think of it as "real care," not a shortcut.

### Objection → brand answer map
| Objection | Answer the brand makes |
|---|---|
| "Is this even legit?" | Licensed U.S. providers, U.S. pharmacies, real prescriptions. |
| "Will I qualify?" | Provider reviews your intake; prescribed only if appropriate. |
| "Is it safe?" | Prescription medication with provider guidance on risks/side effects. |
| "What's the catch on price?" | Transparent plans; only charged if approved. |
| "Do I have to commit?" | Start monthly; cancel anytime; 3-month is value, not lock-in. |
| "Why not the cheap site?" | A protocol with a provider and support — not a one-off script. |

## 4. Competitive context (for messaging)
Four buckets in the market: storefront/access sellers, structured metabolic care (Found, Calibrate), longevity/biomarker (Function, Superpower, Healthspan), and horizontal incumbents (Hims, Ro). **Basis sits between storefront and structured care:** more credible and premium than access sellers, simpler and more focused than longevity platforms. The wedge: *premium feel + real provider + protocol structure, without clinic-level friction.*

"Why not just…" answers to keep handy:
- *…the cheapest GLP-1 site?* Those optimize for price and volume; Basis optimizes for a guided, supported experience.
- *…Hims/Ro?* Bigger and broader; Basis is focused, premium, and protocol-led.
- *…my regular doctor?* You can — Basis is for people who want a faster, online, GLP-1-focused path with provider oversight.

## 5. Brand pillars

**1. Physician-guided.**
- *Meaning:* a licensed provider actually reviews each patient; nothing is auto-approved.
- *Proof:* provider review step, "prescribed if clinically appropriate," eligibility language.
- *Express it:* "Reviewed by licensed providers." *Avoid:* "instant approval."

**2. Protocol-structured.**
- *Meaning:* more than a script — dosing, titration support, refills, continuity.
- *Proof:* Basis Metabolic protocol, multi-month plans, refill support.
- *Express it:* "A protocol, not a one-off script." *Avoid:* heavy clinical-program framing.

**3. Premium & transparent.**
- *Meaning:* clean experience, clear pricing, U.S. pharmacies, no surprises.
- *Proof:* plan clarity, "only charged if approved," premium design.
- *Express it:* "Clear pricing. No surprises." *Avoid:* discount/coupon energy.

**4. Built to grow with you.** *(use sparingly — one line max; the only Phase 2 nod)*
- *Meaning:* Basis can expand into other protocols over time.
- *Express it:* "Start with metabolic care; more protocols as you're ready."

## 6. Voice & tone

**Voice principles**
1. **Clear over clever.** Say the thing. Short sentences.
2. **Credible, not clinical-cold.** Warm authority, like a great doctor who respects your time.
3. **Composed, never hypey.** No exclamation-mark energy, no miracle language.
4. **Honest about the process.** "If appropriate," "eligibility required" build trust, not friction.
5. **Premium through restraint.** Confidence comes from white space and precision, not adjectives.

**Tone by context**
- *Hero/marketing:* confident, aspirational-but-grounded.
- *How it works/FAQ:* plain, reassuring, factual.
- *Compliance/legal:* precise, neutral.
- *Email/SMS:* helpful and human, low-pressure.
- *Errors/forms:* calm, blame-free, solution-first.

**Say this / not that (bank)**
| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| Prescribed online if it's clinically appropriate | Guaranteed approval / fast approval |
| Designed for consistency and better value | Lose 20 lbs in 30 days |
| Physician-guided | AI-powered weight loss |
| A protocol, not a one-off script | Cheapest GLP-1 online |
| You're only charged if a provider approves | Risk-free / no risk |
| Personalized dosing guidance | Custom-compounded miracle stack |
| Select protocols, by provider review | Buy peptides now |

**Vocabulary**
- *Use:* protocol, physician-guided, licensed provider, eligibility, clinically appropriate, U.S. pharmacy, personalized dosing, continuity, support.
- *Avoid:* membership (as hero), subscription (as hero), cure, guarantee, miracle, instant, shortcut, hack, shred, melt, skinny.

**Mechanics:** second person ("you"). Contractions ok. Sentences short. Numerals for stats/prices. Title case for nav/buttons; sentence case for body. One idea per sentence.

## 8. Naming system
- **Brand:** *Basis.* Domain basis.care.
- **Protocols:** `Basis [Track]` → **Basis Metabolic** (GLP-1, the hero). Future: Basis Longevity, Basis Hormone, Basis Recovery, Basis Performance. Always provider-gated language for non-GLP-1.

- **Don't:** invent sub-brands, use clinical jargon as product names, or imply restricted-compound availability.

## 9. Visual identity direction

> Directional starting point — refine in design. Goal: premium clinical, Basal/Nouri commercial clarity with a more elevated brand system. Hims/Ro-level polish, more focused.

**Color (suggested, refine):**
- *Primary ink:* deep near-black or deep forest/ink (e.g., `#10231C` / `#121417`) — authority, calm.
- *Background:* warm off-white / bone (e.g., `#F7F4EE`) — premium, clean, not sterile white.
- *Accent (one only):* restrained — muted sage/clinical green (e.g., `#3E5C4B`) or a soft clinical blue. Use sparingly for CTAs/highlights.
- *Neutrals:* warm grays for body text and borders.
- *Rule:* one accent, lots of neutral, generous negative space. No multi-color gradients.

**Typography (Google-Fonts-accessible options):**
- *Headlines:* an editorial serif or refined display — e.g., **Fraunces**, **Newsreader**, or a high-contrast serif — for premium/clinical-credible feel.
- *Body/UI:* a clean neutral sans — e.g., **Inter** or **Söhne-style** grotesque — for clarity.
- *Hierarchy:* large confident H1 (serif), generous line-height body (~1.6), clear size steps. Restrained weight contrast.

**Imagery / art direction:**
- Clean, calm, real: soft natural light, neutral palettes, hands/lifestyle/considered product shots, abstract clinical textures.
- *Avoid:* before/after bodies, scale/measuring-tape clichés, med-spa glam, stocky doctor-with-clipboard, syringe close-ups as hero, supplement-bro aesthetics.

**Layout & components:**
- Generous whitespace; single-column focus on mobile; clear section rhythm.
- *Buttons:* solid accent primary, ghost/outline secondary; large tap targets; consistent label style ("Start assessment").
- *Cards:* soft radius, subtle shadow/border, premium padding; used for plans + protocol tracks.
- *Badges:* understated ("Recommended" on 3-month). No loud "SALE."
- *Icons:* thin-line, consistent set; minimal.
- *Motion:* subtle fades/reveals only; nothing flashy.

**Accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast; visible focus states; readable base font (≥16px); labels on all inputs.

## 10. Compliance & claims language bank

**Approved phrasings:** "if clinically appropriate," "subject to provider review," "eligibility required," "prescribed online," "U.S. pharmacy fulfillment," "individual results vary," "talk to your provider about risks and side effects."

**Banned:** guaranteed results/approval, specific weight-loss numbers, "risk-free," "miracle/cure," fake testimonials or invented stats, implying restricted peptides are freely available, anything stating everyone qualifies.

**Required disclaimers (every relevant page/footer):** prescriptions only if a provider deems treatment appropriate; GLP-1 are prescription drugs with possible side effects; not medical advice; results vary; telehealth/MSO + privacy disclosures; LegitScript seal once filed.

**Process:** all consumer-facing claims route through LegitScript + provider/compliance sign-off before launch. Copy here is written conservatively to clear Meta and LegitScript, but is draft-for-review.

---

## 11. Do / Don't (quick reference)
**Do:** lead with GLP-1; keep it premium and clear; use light protocol language; show provider review and U.S. pharmacy; anchor the 3-month plan; answer objections plainly.
**Don't:** make it look cheap/med-spa/supplement; lead with membership/labs/biomarkers/portal; overpromise outcomes or peptide availability; bury the offer under longevity language; build the Phase 2 care system yet.
